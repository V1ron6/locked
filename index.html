Built to keep you in the zone

Everything runs on your phone. No account, no ads, no network access.

Unlimited schedules

Set days, start and end times (overnight too) and a block list for each. They start automatically, with a heads-up a minute before.

Global or custom block lists

Build one list of distractions and reuse it on every schedule, or pick apps just for one. Edit the global list once and every schedule follows.

Built-in Pomodoro

25 minutes locked in, then a 5-minute break with your apps unlocked. It repeats until the session ends, with a live countdown.

Hard to cheat, easy to leave

Locked apps are covered by a block screen. Ending early takes a 3-second hold plus a puzzle, so it's a decision, not a reflex.

Start now

No schedule needed. Pick a duration, pick the global list or a custom one, and lock in right away.

Always-allowed apps

Phone, emergency and messages stay reachable. Notifications from locked apps wait until your break.

Install in a minute

No compiling and no Play Store. Download the APK straight to your phone.

  1. Download the APKUse the button above on your phone. It's a debug build, about 10 MB.
  2. Open it and allow the installIf Android asks, allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser or files app. Play Protect may show a warning for debug builds; that's expected.
  3. Turn on the accessibility serviceOpen Locked → Finish setup. Locked only looks at which app is in front, never at what's on screen. If the toggle is greyed out: App info → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again.
  4. Create your first scheduleAllow notifications and exact alarms, build your global block list in Settings, then add a schedule.
Updating: installing over an older build only works if it was signed with the same debug key. If Android says “App not installed”, uninstall the old version first (your schedules will be cleared).

All versions

Newest first. Every build is kept, so you can roll back if something misbehaves.
